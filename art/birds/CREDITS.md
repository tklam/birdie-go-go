# Bird photo credits

The bird sprite sheets (`art/birds/out/<species>.png`) and album portraits
(`art/birds/out/album_<species>.png`) are derived from the photos below by
`tools/birdphoto` (background removal, scaling, reduction to the master palette).
The settings for each sprite frame are in `art/birds/sources.yaml`; copies of the
source photos are in `art/birds/selected/<species>/`.

**License of the derived images.** Sprite frames and album portraits derived from
a CC BY-SA photo (2.0, 3.0 or 4.0 below) are themselves licensed under the same
CC BY-SA version, with attribution to the photographer listed below. The one CC0
photos (CC0, public domain) carry no conditions, and CC BY photos need only the
attribution below. A sprite sheet combines frames from several photos, so it
carries all of their attributions and is distributed under CC BY-SA.

Sections from the Eurasian tree sparrow on are generated by
`go run ./tools/birdphoto -credits <species,...>`. Their cut-outs use segmentation
masks (`selected/<species>/*.mask.png`, made with the rembg U²-Net model), and
frames marked "copy of" reuse another frame of the same photo.
