# Sound credits

O Boy's voice (`art/audio/oboy/*.mp3`, shipped as `web/public/assets/audio/`) is
cut from field recordings of wild Pacific Parrotlets (*Forpus coelestis*) on
[xeno-canto](https://xeno-canto.org). Clips were trimmed, high-pass filtered,
faded and normalised. The full source recordings are in `art/audio/oboy/src/`.

**These recordings are licensed CC BY-NC-SA, so the clips are too, and the game may
only be distributed non-commercially while it uses them.**

## Piano

The lesson piano (`art/audio/piano/piano_*.mp3`, shipped as `web/public/assets/audio/`)
uses samples from the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by Alexander Holm, licensed
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), in the compressed per-note
set published by the [Tone.js audio repository](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio)
(every third semitone, C1–C8). The shipped clips are trimmed to 4 seconds, mixed to
mono and faded out; the full downloads are in `art/audio/piano/src/`. Notes between
samples are pitch-shifted from the nearest one.
